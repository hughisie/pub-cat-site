Drafts checked for local publishers

News drafts for your patch, produced overnight and checked before they reach you.

We take the public sources in and around your town, including council notices, police and emergency updates, transport changes, events and the local press, and turn them into news drafts that are ready to edit. Each draft carries links to its sources and marks anything we could not confirm. Your editor reads it, revises it and decides whether it goes out. We never publish anything on your behalf.

There is no call, no meeting and nothing to sign. Email us your town and your website. Within 48 hours we will send 10 recent drafts from Barna.News, our Barcelona site, so you can judge the quality for yourself, and let you know when we can begin work on your town.

How it works

  1. We monitor your area. The engine behind Barna.News, which publishes 25 to 40 Barcelona stories a day, tracks the official and local sources for your town in Spanish, Catalan or English.
  2. We write the drafts and check them. Every draft is written in English, links each source and goes through a claim check. Any point we could not confirm is flagged in the draft, so your editor can see where to look.
  3. Your editor makes the call. Drafts land in your inbox every morning, ready to paste into your own site. You edit, approve or discard them, and publish under your own name and standards.

What each draft includes

  • A headline and a story in plain British English.
  • A link to every source used.
  • Flags on any claim we could not confirm.
  • A picture only where we can demonstrate the right to use it.

What we never do

  • We never publish on your behalf.
  • We never make up bylines or quotes.
  • We never reproduce text from paywalled sources.

Questions

Is this written by AI? Yes. The drafts are produced by software and checked by software, and someone on your side decides what gets published. Barna.News works the same way: a person approves every story before it goes live.

Do I have to speak to anyone? No. It all happens by email, from the samples through to sign-up.

Which areas do you cover? Barcelona for now. We are opening new towns in Spain and the UK one at a time, in the order that publishers request them.
