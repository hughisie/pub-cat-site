Privacy

pub.cat is run by Owen Hughes, who is based in Barcelona, Spain, and is the person responsible for any personal data gathered through the site. Our other websites each carry their own privacy page.

What we collect

No account is needed to use this site, it has no forms, and it sets no cookies of its own. Should you send an email to hi@pub.cat, we retain the message and your email address in order to answer you.

Third parties that load with this website

Your browser downloads fonts from Google Fonts to render this site and, on the home page, code libraries from unpkg. When that happens, those services can see your IP address and browser details. What they log is outside our control.

How long we keep it

Emails are retained for as long as the exchange remains useful and are deleted if you ask.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it erased. Write to hi@pub.cat and we will respond within one month. Complaints can also be made to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.
