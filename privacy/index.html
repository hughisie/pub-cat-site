Privacy

Pub.cat is a company based in Barcelona that operates news brands in English. This page applies to this website, pub.cat. Our other sites each carry their own privacy page.

What we collect

No account is required on this site, there are no forms, and we set no cookies of our own. Should you email us at hi@pub.cat, we retain your message and your email address in order to reply.

Third parties loaded by this page

When this page is displayed, your browser requests fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from unpkg. On those requests, both services are able to see your IP address and browser details. What they record is outside our control.

How long we keep it

Emails are retained for as long as the correspondence remains useful, and are deleted if you request it.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to request a copy of the data we hold on you, to have it corrected, or to have it erased. Write to hi@pub.cat and we will respond within one month.
